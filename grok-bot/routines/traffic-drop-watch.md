**Schedule:** daily, 08:30 (user's timezone)

**Prompt:**

Check my site's Search Console data via SEOmatic for anomalies in the last
48 hours: any page or query cluster whose clicks or impressions fell
sharply versus its trailing 14-day average. If you find one, diagnose the
likeliest cause (position drop vs impression drop vs CTR drop) and tell me
what to check. If everything is within normal range, reply exactly:
"All quiet - no traffic anomalies." and nothing else.
