# SEOmatic Grok Bot templates

Ready-to-build [Grok Bot](https://x.ai/bot) templates on the SEOmatic MCP server. Install any of them in one click from the Install column, then connect SEOmatic once ([SETUP.md](../SETUP.md)). Each file also has the Bot name, description, instructions, skills and routines if you would rather build it yourself.

| Template | What it does | Grok Bot |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [SEO Teammate](./seo-teammate.md) | The all-round SEO bot: a Monday review, a daily traffic-drop watch and a Thursday striking-distance sweep on your Search Console data, with fixes staged for your approval. | [Install](https://x.ai/bot/TAd_1q_rpYCcb94h8PXDG) |
| [Weekly SEO Brief](./weekly-seo-brief.md) | Every Monday, a five-line brief of what changed in your Google Search Console and what to do this week. | [Install](https://x.ai/bot/EkSJB1aArC2kU3odN1Z5W) |
| [Traffic Drop Watch](./traffic-drop-watch.md) | Watches your Search Console every morning and only speaks when clicks fall, with the likely cause and what to check. | [Install](https://x.ai/bot/9pxPIxJf0M10hPq1e4Jt4) |
| [Quick Wins Hunter](./quick-wins-hunter.md) | Finds keywords one push from page 1 and pages that rank but lose the click, every week, and stages the fixes for your approval. | [Install](https://x.ai/bot/3DxcsWM7WiqsIrWHlH3AS) |
| [AI Visibility Watch](./ai-visibility-watch.md) | Tracks how ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews describe your brand, which AI bots crawl you, and what changed. | [Install](https://x.ai/bot/yWUO_uI9JSZEXgYPsIzXx) |
| [Local SEO Desk](./local-seo-desk.md) | For local businesses: watches your Google Business Profile reviews, map-pack positions and local searches, and proposes the next fix. | [Install](https://x.ai/bot/0_GWfmGaBw-kQC856K2fo) |
| [Agency Reporter](./agency-reporter.md) | Writes the monthly SEO report for each client from real Search Console data: results, what changed and whether it worked, AI search, next month. | [Install](https://x.ai/bot/u8hSS6eJdCwrnZXBcwtw8) |
| [Content Planner](./content-planner.md) | Plans what to write and what to update from your own Search Console data, then writes a brief for each piece. | [Install](https://x.ai/bot/65wGaCazNY1t1K3I4oFy0) |
| [Technical SEO Monitor](./technical-seo-monitor.md) | Checks indexing, sitemaps, broken links and noindex leaks on your important pages every week, and stages fixes. | [Install](https://x.ai/bot/OnUJbQKiams1O9GcQR1BN) |

Every template needs the one-time SEOmatic connection in [SETUP.md](../SETUP.md).
