# SEOmatic Grok Bot templates

Ready-to-build [Grok Bot](https://x.ai/bot) templates on the SEOmatic MCP server. Each file has the Bot name, description, instructions, skills and routines; build it once, then **Share as template**.

| Template | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [SEO Teammate](../README.md) | The all-round template: weekly review, drop watch, quick wins |
| [Weekly SEO Brief](./weekly-seo-brief.md) | Every Monday, a five-line brief of what changed in your Google Search Console and what to do this week. |
| [Traffic Drop Watch](./traffic-drop-watch.md) | Watches your Search Console every morning and only speaks when clicks fall, with the likely cause and what to check. |
| [Quick Wins Hunter](./quick-wins-hunter.md) | Finds keywords one push from page 1 and pages that rank but lose the click, every week, and stages the fixes for your approval. |
| [AI Visibility Watch](./ai-visibility-watch.md) | Tracks how ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews describe your brand, which AI bots crawl you, and what changed. |
| [Local SEO Desk](./local-seo-desk.md) | For local businesses: watches your Google Business Profile reviews, map-pack positions and local searches, and proposes the next fix. |
| [Agency Reporter](./agency-reporter.md) | Writes the monthly SEO report for each client from real Search Console data: results, what changed and whether it worked, AI search, next month. |
| [Content Planner](./content-planner.md) | Plans what to write and what to update from your own Search Console data, then writes a brief for each piece. |
| [Technical SEO Monitor](./technical-seo-monitor.md) | Checks indexing, sitemaps, broken links and noindex leaks on your important pages every week, and stages fixes. |

Every template needs the one-time SEOmatic connection in [SETUP.md](../SETUP.md).
